✦01 / PREVENTIVE

Dental Check-up

A routine examination to assess your oral health and identify concerns early.

₹500 / visit

Before: Brush gently and bring any previous dental reports or medication details.

After: Follow the dentist's recommendations and schedule the next review if advised.

◈02 / PREVENTIVE

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling to remove plaque and hardened deposits from teeth.

₹1,200 / session

Before: Eat normally and let the dentist know about sensitivity or gum concerns.

After: Brush gently, maintain daily flossing, and avoid very hot or cold foods if sensitive.

✧03 / COSMETIC

Teeth Whitening

A cosmetic treatment designed to brighten the appearance of your smile.

₹3,500 / session

Before: Get a dental assessment first; whitening may not suit every patient.

After: Follow the dentist's advice and limit staining foods and drinks as instructed.

⌁04 / RESTORATIVE

Dental Filling

Repairing a tooth affected by decay or minor damage to restore its function.

₹1,000 / tooth

Before: Share allergies, medicines, and any pain or sensitivity with the dentist.

After: Wait until numbness wears off before eating; contact the clinic if your bite feels uneven.

◎05 / RESTORATIVE

Root Canal

Treatment to address infection or inflammation inside a tooth and help preserve it.

₹4,500 / tooth*

Before: Share your symptoms and medical history; take medicines only as prescribed.

After: Avoid chewing on the treated tooth until restored, and attend all follow-up visits.

⌖06 / SURGICAL

Tooth Extraction

Careful removal of a tooth when it cannot be preserved or is causing problems.

₹1,500 / tooth*

Before: Disclose all medicines and health conditions; follow eating instructions from the clinic.

After: Follow the dentist's written instructions. Avoid smoking, straws, and vigorous rinsing initially.

*Starting prices shown for demonstration only. Final costs depend on examination, case complexity, materials, and treatment plan. These general notes do not replace personalised advice from a qualified dentist.
